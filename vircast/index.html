The usual ways of sending audio between PCs fight you.

Every 2-PC streamer has tried at least one of these. Here's what goes wrong, and what VirCast does instead.

The usual wayVirCast Direct
Line-in cableA 3.5 mm cable from one PC's output to the other's input.
→

VBAN and network mixersStream names and IP addresses typed on both PCs.
→

It finds the other PC by name and recommends the right address.

3

Press Start on both

One button starts the lane on this PC and the other one.

Six lanes. Each color plugs into the same color.

Set each lane to send or receive, pick its device, and the matching lane on the other PC does the opposite. Tap a cable to mute it.

Signal on each cable flows in the direction that lane sends.

  • Its own device per laneSend system audio, a single output, or a mic. Receive into any output, including Wave Link.
  • Mute, mono and boostFix a one-sided mic with one press, or push a quiet source up.
  • A meter and live statsSee the level, data rate and any missing audio for every lane.
  • Listen testHear exactly what a lane is sending on your own headphones.

A mic chain, built in.

Run your microphone through VirCast's local mixer before it goes anywhere. Pick a preset or shape it yourself.

  • Presets: Clean, Broadcast and Warm
  • EQ, background-noise gate, compressor and limiter
  • Reverb and echo when you want them
  • Changes play instantly, and Cancel restores what you had

Out of the way while you play.

Shrink VirCast to a mini bar that stays on top of your game. Close the window and it tucks into the tray, and the audio keeps running.

It stays on your network.

VirCast only talks to PCs in your home. There's no account, no cloud and nothing to upload.

  • Local network onlyInternet and public addresses are refused, so audio can't leave your house by accident.
  • No cloud, no trackingThere's no telemetry. The traffic numbers you see are counted on your PC and never sent anywhere.
  • Remembers your PCA paired PC is trusted, and VirCast fixes the connection itself if your router hands out a new address.
